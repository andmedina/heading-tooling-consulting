03 / Production data & analytics

Make production data
useful on the shop floor.

When shift reports, downtime notes, and reject counts live in separate places, patterns can be hard to see. We help turn that information into a clear view of what deserves attention.

Explore the dashboard ↓
From problem to practical support

Better questions.
Clearer production insight.

  • “We have spreadsheets, but no shared picture.”Review existing sources, align definitions, and design a report that brings the key production measures together.
  • “We know we lose time, but not where to start.”Organize stop records into useful categories and surface patterns that can guide a focused review with the production team.
  • “Reports take too long to prepare.”Map repetitive reporting steps and identify opportunities to standardize or automate them, based on the systems already in use.
  • “We changed something. Did it help?”Define a baseline, consistent measures, and a comparison period so a change can be reviewed with appropriate production context.
WHAT WE DELIVER

A reporting setup your team can use

  1. A map of available reports, spreadsheets, and information gaps.
  2. Agreed definitions for output, downtime, and reject measures.
  3. A dashboard or reporting workflow focused on your production questions.
  4. A prioritized list of findings and follow-up checks.
  5. A practical handover explaining how the report is updated and interpreted.
